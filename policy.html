Sprinkle Sprinkle Policy

Please read the following information before submitting an enquiry with Sprinkle Sprinkle.

Orders and Enquiries

All enquiries are subject to availability and confirmation by Sprinkle Sprinkle. Submitting an enquiry does not automatically confirm an order.

Order Lead Times

Customers are encouraged to submit enquiries as early as possible, especially for customised cakes, large orders and special events. Availability depends on the requested date and existing bookings.

Deposits and Payments

A deposit may be required to secure an order, particularly for customised cakes and larger orders. The required deposit amount will be communicated to the customer once the enquiry has been reviewed and the order details have been confirmed.

An order will only be considered confirmed once the required deposit has been received and confirmation has been provided by Sprinkle Sprinkle. Any outstanding balance must be settled according to the payment arrangements communicated to the customer.

Custom Designs and Inspiration Images

Customers may provide an inspiration image and describe their preferred colours, themes and designs. Inspiration images are used as a guide and the final product may vary slightly from the reference image.

Sprinkle Sprinkle will make every effort to create the requested design while considering the agreed colours, theme and available decorating materials.

Allergies and Dietary Requirements

Customers must inform Sprinkle Sprinkle of any allergies or dietary requirements when submitting an enquiry.

Although care is taken when preparing products, the kitchen may handle common allergens. Customers are responsible for providing accurate information about their dietary requirements.

Delivery and Collection

Customers can choose between delivery and collection when submitting an enquiry. Delivery and collection arrangements will be confirmed once the order has been reviewed and accepted.

Customers who choose delivery must provide a complete and accurate delivery address.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges are based on the customer's delivery location and will be communicated when the enquiry is reviewed. The delivery fee may vary depending on the distance and location of the order.

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate delivery address so that the appropriate delivery charge can be determined.

Cancellations and Refunds

Customers who need to cancel an order are requested to provide at least seven days' notice before the scheduled order date.

Where an order is cancelled at least seven days before the scheduled date, the deposit may be eligible for a refund, subject to the circumstances of the order and any costs already incurred by Sprinkle Sprinkle.

Cancellations made less than seven days before the scheduled order date may result in the deposit being non-refundable, particularly where ingredients, decorations or other materials have already been purchased or preparation has begun.

If Sprinkle Sprinkle is unable to fulfil a confirmed order, the customer will be contacted as soon as possible and any applicable refund will be discussed with the customer.

Changes to Orders

Customers should contact Sprinkle Sprinkle as soon as possible if they need to make changes to an enquiry or confirmed order.

Changes to confirmed orders may not always be possible and may result in additional charges depending on the nature of the requested changes.

Product Variations

Sprinkle Sprinkle products are handmade and slight variations in colour, decoration, size or appearance may occur. These variations are a natural part of handmade baking and do not affect the quality of the product.

Customer Agreement

By submitting an enquiry, customers confirm that the information they have provided is accurate and that they have read and understood the Sprinkle Sprinkle Policy.
