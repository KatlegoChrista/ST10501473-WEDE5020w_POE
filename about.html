About Sprinkle Sprinkle

Sprinkle Sprinkle is a small baking business specialising in personalised cakes and freshly baked treats for special moments. We create each treat with care and creativity to help make your special moments memorable.

Our mission is to create delicious goodies that help make every milestone memorable. Our goal is to grow into private event catering and create opportunities within our community.

Whether you are celebrating something big or simply satisfying your sweet tooth, we at Sprinkle Sprinkle are here to sprinkle a little sweetness to your day.
